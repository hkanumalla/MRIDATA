Raw dimensions (paste CVI)
Formatted — edit numbers here

Paste raw CVI on the left; it auto-formats into the editable box on the right. Adjust or round the numbers there — the z-scores in the report are computed from the edited box. Recognized (with z-scores): aortic root, ascending aorta, distal transverse aortic arch, aortic isthmus, main / right / left pulmonary artery. Aortic segments without a Boston regression (descending / diaphragmatic / distal ascending) are reformatted without a z-score. Unlabeled blocks (e.g. a header followed by 1–3 lengths) prompt you to assign them (usually aortic root). Average gives one z from the mean of the dimensions; Individual gives a z for each. The aortic root always shows all its dimensions and its z is taken from the maximal dimension (label and notes such as “(systole)” or “(from sinus to commissure)” are preserved). Inline perimeter (e.g. “…cm, peri 100.5 mm”) is split onto its own field and excluded from the diameter z. BSA is taken from the pasted ventricular data (absolute ÷ indexed) if present, otherwise type it above.
